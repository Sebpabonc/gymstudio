## Findings

### [P2] Progress: "Add weight" suggestions point at the ended block, so Apply has no visible effect — covered by #85
- Where: Progress > Suggestions, then Planned before you go, demo mode, 375 px (also 768 / desktop).
- Steps: 1. Open Progress. 2. Tap Apply on "Ready to add 2.5 kg on Barbell Bench Press (Incline) next B day." 3. Open Planned before you go (Block 6 is selected by default because it starts tomorrow) and open every day.
- Expected: the suggestion targets an exercise in the upcoming/current block, and the next matching B day shows "+2.5 kg applied" with the raised weight.
- Actual: all 5 suggestions are Block 5 exercises (Barbell Bench Press (Incline) and (Decline), Cable Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip), Cable Straight-Arm Pulldown, Cable Seated Row (Single-Arm)). None of them are in Block 6, so after Apply no day in the default block shows the chip. Progress treats this week as "Block 5" (`currentOrLatestBlock`), while Planned shows Block 6 (`defaultActiveBlock` picks the next block in a gap week).
- The feature does work when the matching block is selected. After switching Planned to Block 5, Day 4 (Chest-Back B) showed "+2.5 KG APPLIED" with Set 1 at 65 kg (62.5 + 2.5), and Day 1 (A) was unchanged. Applied ✓ / Undo also work: Undo clears the target.
- No new issue: #85 already says "suggestions scoped to the current block only (fix: they still point at ended Block 5)". #77 covers making block status consistent between screens.
