## Findings
### [P2] Today: next-session recommendation says "reduce weight" when sets are simply not logged yet
- Where: Today, Block 6 Day 1, A1 Dumbbell Bench Press (Incline), 375 px, demo, EN and ES
- Steps: 1. Open `?demo=1`. 2. Expand A1 (1 of 3 sets logged today: 20 kg × 8, target 8·8·8).
- Expected: no "reduce" advice; unlogged sets are not "short" (or no recommendation until the session is complete).
- Actual: "Next: 17.5 kg × 8 reps · 3 sets — Two or more sets were at least 3 reps short; reduce the weight by 5–10%". The inputs are prefilled with 17.5 kg.
- Cause: `src/progress/nextTarget.ts` `recommendNextTarget` counts missing sets as 0 reps (`loggedSets[index]?.reps ?? 0`).
