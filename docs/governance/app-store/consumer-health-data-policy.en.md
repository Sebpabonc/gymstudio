# GymStudio Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [PO: publication date]

This policy is for people in the United States, including Washington and Nevada. It is run by Sebastian Pabon, js.pabonc979@gmail.com.

## Health data we collect

Body weight, steps and calories you type in; workouts, sets and loads; your training goal, such as losing fat or gaining muscle; experience, activity level and equipment; and
notes you add about your goal.

## Why we collect it and how we use it

To build and adapt your plan, recommend today's load, show your progress and sync your data across your devices. If you turn on AI, also to explain your loads, personalise your plan and
answer your questions.

## Where it comes from

From you, and from what the app calculates from what you log.

## Who receives it

We do not sell it. We share it with no third parties or affiliates, except where you ask or the law requires. These service providers process it for us, under our instructions:

|---|---|
| Hosting | Supabase |
| AI text, only if you turn on AI | OpenAI |

## Your rights

You can see your health data and who received it, withdraw consent and delete it.

- **Delete:** Profile → Delete account in the app [REQUIRES G1]. Backups expire within [VERIFY: 7 to 30 days].
- **Withdraw consent for AI:** Profile → Privacy & AI [REQUIRES G3].
- **Anything else:** write to js.pabonc979@gmail.com from your account's email address. We answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we'll tell you in that period, and take at most 45 more.
- **Appeal:** if we refuse, reply to our answer within a reasonable time. We decide within 45 days and explain why. If you disagree, you can complain to your state's attorney general.
