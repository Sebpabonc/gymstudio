# GymStudio support

**Contact:** gymstudioapp@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 5 business days. Tell us your device, iOS version (or browser) and what you
were doing. Never send us your password.

Signed in, you can also write to us from the app: tap the **Help and AI** button, then **Send feedback**.

**Medical emergencies:** we can't help. Call your local emergency number.

## Delete your account

Today, account deletion works by email:

1. Write to **gymstudioapp@gmail.com** from your GymStudio account's email address (for Google sign-in, your Google email), with the
   subject "Delete my account".
2. We may ask you to confirm the request, to make sure it comes from you.
3. We delete your account and all its data within **10 days** and email you when it is done.

Removing the app alone does not delete your account. Data on your phone stays until you remove the app. Details are in the
[Privacy Policy](https://sebpabonc.github.io/gymstudio/privacy/). We are adding a delete button inside the app; this page will say so
when it is available.

## Turn AI off

AI is optional and off until you tap **Allow AI**. To turn it off later, go to **You → Privacy & AI** and switch off **AI features
(OpenAI)**. The app keeps working with rule-based reasons and plans. See the [Privacy Policy](https://sebpabonc.github.io/gymstudio/privacy/)
for what AI sends to OpenAI.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?** Not to try GymStudio; what you log then stays on your device. Plans, goals, measurements, sync, feedback and AI need an
account. You can sign up with email or with Google.

**Does it work offline?** Logging does, and your data syncs when you reconnect. Sign-in, new plans and AI need a connection.

**I didn't get the confirmation or reset email.** It comes from gymstudioapp@gmail.com. Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes.
Still nothing? Write to us.

**I forgot my password.** On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**. We email you a link to set a new password.

**How is today's weight chosen?** The app's rules use your recent sets. Change it if it doesn't feel right. You decide what you lift.

**Is a person coaching me?** No. GymStudio's AI trainer is software. No human trainer or health professional reviews or supervises your
plan. The app's rules and plan templates were prepared with an AI specialist agent and approved by the app's owner.

**Is this medical advice?** No. Check with a doctor before starting a program, and stop if you feel pain or unwell.

**Why doesn't the timer show on my Lock Screen?** Live Activities need an iPhone with iOS 16.2 or later, and they must be allowed for the
app in iOS Settings. The Dynamic Island appears on iPhone models that have it.

**Which devices are supported?** iPhone with iOS 15 or later, and the web app at https://sebpabonc.github.io/gymstudio/ in a current
browser. There is no iPad version.

**Is GymStudio free?** Yes. There are no payments or subscriptions in the app.

**Who can use it?** People aged 18 or older.
