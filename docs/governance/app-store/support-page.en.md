## Delete your account

1. Open GymStudio and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Delete account**. [REQUIRES G1]
3. Confirm.

Your account and associated data are removed permanently from our database right away. Backups expire within [VERIFY: 7 to 30 days]. Deleting the
app alone does not delete your account. Data stays on another phone until you open the app there or remove it. **Can't sign in?** Email

## Turn AI off

**Profile → Privacy & AI.** [REQUIRES G3] The app keeps working: you see rule-based reasons and plans. See the [Privacy Policy](https://sebpabonc.github.io/gymstudio/legal/privacy.en.html).

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?** No, to try the demo. Plans, goals, measurements, sync and AI need one.

**Does it work offline?** Logging does; your data syncs when you reconnect. Sign-in, new plans and AI need a connection.

**I didn't get the confirmation or reset email.** Check spam, and wait a few minutes. [REQUIRES G6, G10]

**How is today's weight chosen?** The app's rules use your recent sets. Change it if it doesn't feel right. You decide what you lift.

**Is a person coaching me?** No. GymStudio's AI trainer is software. No human trainer or health professional reviews your plan. The app's rules and plan templates were prepared with an AI specialist agent and approved by the app's owner.

**Is this medical advice?** No. Check with a doctor before starting a program, and stop if you feel pain or unwell.

**Why doesn't the timer show on my Lock Screen?** Live Activities need an iPhone with iOS 16.2 or later and must be on in iOS Settings → GymStudio → Live Activities.
The Dynamic Island appears on models that have it.

**Is GymStudio free?** Yes, at launch.
