## 1. Ownership and commercial use

- ToS §4.4: Kling says it does not claim ownership of user content (input or output). Rights sit with the
  user or the rightful holder under law. Whether AI output is protected by copyright at all depends on
  local law (likely weak or none in the US for purely AI-made output). **Lawyer.**
- ToS §4.6: the general rule is that commercial use of output needs Kling's written permission.
- Paid Terms §3.1.2 overrides this for members: commercial use of output by members "is not restricted",
  including use, reproduction, distribution, modification and derivative works. Only exception:
  developing or offering "competitive products or services of KLING AI". A gym app is not a video-generation
  service, so this exception should not apply. `⚠️ VERIFY` with a lawyer if GymStudio ever adds its own
  AI video generation.
- **Timing risk.** The commercial-use benefit is listed among benefits "during the Validity Period" of the
  membership, and §3.1.5 says benefits end when the membership lapses. The Paid Terms do not say whether
  videos made while subscribed stay commercially usable after cancelling. `⚠️ VERIFY` / **Lawyer**:
  either keep the plan active while the videos are in the app, or get written confirmation from Kling
  (PO's decision; Governancer does not contact Kling).
- Recommendation: keep a log per video (date generated, plan active that day, Kling task id, credits).

## 2. Watermark

- Paid Terms §3.1.2 and §7.2: watermark removal is a member benefit; members hold the right to remove
  brand watermarks from their content.
- Non-members must label output with Kling's brand, or, where that is impossible, state clearly that the
  output was generated by Kling AI (ToS §4.5, Paid Terms §7.2).
- Community Guidelines §III(VIII)6 forbids removing, altering or hiding **AI-generated content labels**.
  This is separate from the brand watermark. `⚠️ VERIFY` whether Pro downloads carry an invisible or
  metadata AI label (for example C2PA/content credentials); if so, do not strip it in editing.
- Recommendation regardless of terms: tell users in the app that technique videos are AI-generated
  (see `docs/governance/app-store/` AI disclosure).

## 3. Rights we grant Kling over uploads (Seb's photos, elements, motion clips)

- ToS §4.7.1: a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence over input **and** output to host, store, display,
  reproduce, modify, make derivative works and distribute in any media, for the "operational period of
  its business" (long-lived, not stated as perpetual).
- ToS §4.7.2: Kling may license content to third parties.
- ToS §4.7.3(f): Kling may use content to create, train and improve its AI models.
- ToS §4.7.4: authorisation can be revoked by emailing support; effect on past use is not stated.
- Privacy Policy: for portrait features it may extract facial feature points and contours, yet also says
  it does not collect or store face data. The two statements conflict. `⚠️ VERIFY`.
- Deletion: via support@kling.ai or in-app account deletion (Privacy Policy). Elements are not mentioned
  by name. `⚠️ VERIFY` that an element can be deleted in the app and what happens to its source photos.
- **Consequence for Seb's consent.** Uploading his photos grants Kling (and possibly its licensees) these
  rights, including model training, and the data is stored in Singapore. Seb's written consent should
  cover this explicitly, not only "AI videos of me in GymStudio". **Lawyer** (GDPR-style biometric/likeness
  rules if Seb or users are in the EU; Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 on personal data).
- **Stock motion clips.** Uploading a Pexels clip with an identifiable person also grants Kling the licence
  above, including training. Our ToS warranty (§4.3) says we hold authorisation for every input. Whether
  the Pexels License lets us pass that on is unclear. `⚠️ VERIFY` / **Lawyer**.

## 4. Real people's likeness

- No Kling clause bans generating real people with consent. There is no explicit deepfake or consent rule
  for depicting real people outside sexual content.
- What applies: ToS §4.3 (user warrants it has authorisation for the input and is liable for infringement);
  ToS §3.1(i) and (p) (no impersonating anyone; no infringing portrait or privacy rights); Paid Terms
  §7.3.5 and §7.4 (user responsible for third-party rights, including portrait rights); Community
  Guidelines §II.1 and §III(IV)1 (respect likeness, reputation, privacy).
- So the risk sits with us: we need provable consent from Seb (and from anyone else whose face could
  appear). `⚠️ VERIFY` a signed, dated release exists; it is not in the repo and must not be (personal data).
  **Lawyer** for the release text (scope: in-app, marketing, AI training by Kling, duration, withdrawal).
- Third parties: output must not reproduce the stock-clip person's face, a real gym's branding or
  recognisable trademarks (ToS §3.1(p), Apple 5.2.1). QAer/PT to check each video.

## 5. Anything that blocks App Store use

No blocker found in Kling's terms, on these conditions:

1. Plan is a paid membership when each video is generated (commercial use and watermark removal).
2. The commercial-use-after-cancellation question is settled (section 1) or the plan stays active.
3. Apple 5.2.2 says an app using content from a third-party service must be specifically permitted under
   that service's terms, and authorisation "must be provided upon request". Keep a PDF/print of the Paid
   Terms version in force and the plan receipt outside the repo, ready for App Review. **PO**.
4. Apple 5.2.1 (no protected third-party material) and 2.3.9 (rights to all materials in screenshots and
   previews): applies if Kling clips are used in App Store previews too (Socialer).
5. Apple 5.1.2(i) concerns sharing **users'** data with third-party AI. We do not send user data to Kling,
   so this does not apply to the videos. `⚠️ VERIFY` with the Tech Lead that no user data ever goes to Kling.
6. Videos are bundled or streamed as our own static content, not generated live in the app, so Apple 1.2
   (UGC / AI content creation apps) should not apply. `⚠️ VERIFY` with the Tech Lead.
7. Kling ToS §3.1(g) forbids incorporating "the Services" into another product. Shipping finished output
   files is output use, not service embedding. Calling the Kling API from the app would be different.
