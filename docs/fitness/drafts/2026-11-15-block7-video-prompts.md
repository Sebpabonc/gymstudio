## Summary
Seventeen 5-second Kling prompts, one per exercise in block 7 "Strength · Heavy Sets of 6-8 II"
(`block-2026-11-16-strength-hypertrophy-ii`, starts 2026-11-16). `incline-dumbbell-press` and
`dumbbell-bench-press` are left out because they already have approved clips. The movement text comes only
from the approved `posture_tips` and `squeeze_cue` in `docs/fitness/approved/catalogue-v2/*.json`, plus the
block notes where they are more specific (RDL 3 s lowering, chest-supported row at 30 degrees, calf pause at the
bottom). Where a tip leaves a visual detail open, the PT picks the standard, safe option and lists it under
**[PT choice]**. That tag stays out of the prompt so Kling never sees it.

Settings for every clip: Kling 4.0 Omni reference, 1080p, 5 s, 1:1, reference element = "Seb" (the real Seb,
with his own arm and leg tattoos kept).

The prompts use the format of the standing-curl prompt Sebas liked
(`docs/fitness/drafts/2026-11-15-kling-standing-curl.md`), with these lessons built into every prompt:
- **Look:** dark matte black studio, dark rubber floor, soft studio light, plain black clothes with no logos, and
  the real equipment the exercise needs (bench, rack, Smith machine, cable tower, machine; all unbranded), so he
  is never floating against an empty wall.
- **Framing (failed in 2 of 3 takes):** tripod about 4 m away; whole body centred, about 75% of the frame height;
  space above the head; floor visible below the feet; head and face never cut off. For lying, seated, hanging
  and kneeling exercises, the whole body and the whole piece of equipment are in frame.
- **No watch:** "bare wrists, no watch, no bracelet, no earrings" comes in the first sentence.
- **Tempo:** exactly one rep on a 5 s timeline. The lowering (eccentric) is always slower than the lift. The last
  0.4 s or more is still and matches the first frame.
- **Grip and joints:** described positively and more than once, plus one "NOT ..." aimed at that exercise's
  typical AI failure.
- **Uncommon machines** (standing leg curl, Smith calf raise, Bayesian cable curl, and the Smith bench and seated
  press bench too) are described piece by piece.
- **Camera:** three-quarter front for most exercises. Side views are turned 15-30 degrees toward his front for
  hinges, squats, rows and lying lifts, so the key joint shows and his face stays visible. No pure profiles.

How the PT reviews a take: every item in the exercise's Check list must pass, and an item marked (critical)
means automatic rejection. These standard checks apply to every clip and are not repeated below:
- exactly one rep, with the first and last frames matching;
- he reads as Seb: same face and beard, his real tattoos, no cap;
- bare wrists, no jewellery;
- head, feet and all the equipment in frame;
- no text, numbers or logos anywhere;
- dark studio look.

Length: every prompt is one line, under 1,900 characters. I checked each with a line-length search; the
measured bands are listed under each prompt.
