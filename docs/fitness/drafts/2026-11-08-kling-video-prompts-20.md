## Summary
Prompts for 20 five-second Kling 4.0 technique clips, one exercise each, with the assigned athlete's reference
element. The movement text comes only from the approved `posture_tips` in `docs/fitness/approved/catalogue-v2/*.json`,
written as what the camera sees. Where a tip leaves a visual detail open, the standard, safe option is chosen and
listed under **[PT choice]** (kept out of the prompt text so Kling never sees the tag). Tips about load choice,
pain or getting on and off equipment are not filmed. Each prompt is one line and at most 1,900 characters.

Camera rule (PO correction, 2026-11-08): each angle is the one that best shows the technique; a true side view is
used where it shows the technique best. Never from behind. The face does not have to be visible in every frame, but
the athlete must be clearly recognisable (face, head and build sharp and well lit whenever the angle allows), and
the head is never cut off.

How the PT reviews a take: every exercise's Check list must pass. Items marked (critical) mean an automatic reject.
Standard checks for every clip (not repeated below): exactly one rep; first and last frames match; head, feet and
the whole machine or bench in frame; athlete clearly recognisable as the reference person; not filmed from behind;
no text, numbers or logos on plates, machines or clothes.
