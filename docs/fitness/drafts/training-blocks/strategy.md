# Training strategy — 2026/27 blocks

## The weekly split and why

Each week has six sessions, done in this order:

1. **Chest-Back A**
2. **Arms A** (shoulders, biceps, triceps)
3. **Lower Body A** (quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves, core)
4. **Chest-Back B**
5. **Arms B**
6. **Lower Body B**

Every muscle is trained **twice a week**, about 3–4 days apart. Twice a week is a well-supported
frequency for building muscle, and the gap between sessions gives each muscle time to recover.
Pairing chest with back means pushing and pulling get the same attention, which keeps the shoulders
balanced. Core work sits at the end of the lower-body days.

## A days vs B days

The B day repeats the **same exercises, in the same order, with the same method** as its A day,
but with **more reps and therefore lighter weights**:

| A day | B day |
|---|---|
| flat 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 | flat 8 / 10 / 12 / 15 |
| reverse pyramid 8-10-12-15 | 10-12-15-20 |
| ascending pyramid 15-12-10-8 | 20-15-12-10 |
| drop set 12+12 (or 15+15) | 15+15 (or 20+20) |

The A day is the heavier, mechanical-tension day; the B day adds volume and a pump with less stress
on joints. Together they cover both ends of the muscle-building rep range every week.

## Training methods explained

Throughout, "failure" means the point where you can't do another clean rep. "RPE 9" means you
finish a set feeling you had about one more good rep in you.

**Flat pyramid (straight sets).** Same reps and same weight on every set. Example (A day):
4 x 12 at 20 kg, 20 kg, 20 kg, 20 kg. Weight stays constant inside the session and goes up from
week to week. *Goal:* accumulate quality volume. *Rest:* 60–90 s. *Where:* everywhere in a
volume block (coach's block 1).

**Ascending pyramid** (`pyramid` in the app). Reps go **down** while the weight goes **up** every
set. Example (A day): 15 reps at 40 kg → 12 at 45 kg → 10 at 50 kg → 8 at 55 kg. The light first
sets act as a built-in warm-up and the last set is the heaviest. *Goal:* volume plus a heavy top
set. *Rest:* 90–120 s. *Where:* only on the first big lift for each muscle (coach's block 4).

**Reverse pyramid** (`reverse-pyramid`). The opposite: **heaviest set first** at the lowest reps,
then the weight comes **down** while reps go up. Example (A day): 8 reps at 60 kg → 10 at 55 kg
→ 12 at 50 kg → 15 at 42.5 kg. Warm up properly before set 1, because it is the hardest. *Goal:*
lift your heaviest weight while fresh, then pile on fatigue with lighter sets. *Rest:* 90 s.
*Where:* the first big lift for each muscle (coach's blocks 2 and 5).

**Drop set** (`drop-set`). Written as "12+12": do 12 reps at a weight that brings you close to
failure, immediately reduce the weight by about 20–30%, and do 12 more **without resting**. That
is one set. Example: 3 sets of 12 at 30 kg + 12 at 22 kg. *Goal:* extra fatigue and pump with
little joint stress. *Rest:* 90 s after the pair. *Where:* machines, cables and isolation exercises
in the middle or end of the session, never on heavy barbell lifts (coach's blocks 2 and 5).

**Superset** (`superset`). Two exercises with the same letter (B1 + B2) done back to back:
B1, rest about 10 s, B2, then rest 60–90 s and repeat. The coach usually paired a compound (a press,
a pulldown) with an isolation for the same muscle (a fly, a pullover). Example: B1 10 reps of
incline dumbbell press, 10 s, B2 12 reps of incline flies, 90 s rest, x3. *Goal:* more work in less
time and a strong pump. *Where:* after the first main lift (coach's blocks 1 and 4).

**Strength-hypertrophy ("functional hypertrophy").** Low reps (6 on A, 8 on B) with heavy weights on
compound lifts, flat sets, and longer rests of about 2 minutes. Example: 4 x 6 at 80 kg. Fewer
exercises (5–6 per day), no supersets or drop sets. *Goal:* raise strength so the weights used in the
next hypertrophy blocks are higher. *Where:* the first two or three lifts of the day; lighter
accessories follow at 8–10 reps (coach's block 3).

**Session order (all blocks).** Big compound lifts first while you are fresh, then supersets, then
isolation and drop sets, then calves and core last.

## How to progress inside a block (week to week)

- Keep the same exercises for all six weeks so progress is measurable.
- When you hit **every rep of every set** with good form at **RPE 9 or easier**, add the smallest
  step next time (about 2.5% or 1–2.5 kg on upper body, 2.5–5 kg on lower body).
- If you miss reps, keep the weight and beat it next week (one more rep counts as progress).
- In pyramids, progress each set's weight separately. For drop sets, raise the starting weight.
- The B day is progressed the same way, independently of the A day.

## The coach's five blocks (Feb–Sep 2026)

| # | Start | Method | A / B reps | Techniques | What it targets |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 16 Feb | Flat pyramid | 10–12 / 12–15 | Supersets | Volume base |
| 2 | 30 Mar | Reverse pyramid | 8→15 / 10→20 | Drop sets | Intensity, fatigue |
| 3 | 11 May | Strength-hypertrophy | 6–8 / 8–10 | Straight heavy sets | Strength on compounds |
| 4 | 6 Jul | Ascending pyramid | 15→8 / 20→10 | Supersets | Volume with heavy top sets |
| 5 | 17 Aug | Reverse pyramid | 8→15 / 10→20 | Drop sets | Intensity, new exercises |

**The logic across blocks:** the coach alternates a *volume* block with an *intensity* block and
inserts a *heavy strength* block every few blocks. Changing method every 6 weeks gives the body a new
stimulus before progress stalls, while the muscles trained and the split stay the same.

## The new blocks (6–9)

The new blocks continue the same rotation — volume → strength → pyramid volume → reverse-pyramid
intensity — so no two consecutive blocks use the same method. Each block changes most exercises
(machines, cables, dumbbells and barbells, like the coach) so it is not a copy of an earlier one.

- **Block 6 — Hypertrophy Flat Pyramid II (28 Sep 2026).** Volume after the block 5 intensity
  phase. Flat 10–12 reps (A) / 12–15 (B) with supersets. New: pendulum squat, Bulgarian split
  squats, T-bar row, incline machine press, rear-delt work on arms day, cable crunches.
- **Block 7 — Strength-Hypertrophy II (9 Nov 2026).** Heavy block like the coach's block 3:
  flat 6s (A) / 8s (B), 2-minute rests, 5–6 exercises. New: back squat, barbell row, seated barbell
  overhead press, weighted dips and wide pull-ups, JM press, ab wheel.
- **Block 8 — Hypertrophy Pyramid II (21 Dec 2026).** Ascending pyramids 15-12-10-8 (A) /
  20-15-12-10 (B) on the first lift, then supersets. **Week 1 is a lighter week** (it falls on the
  holidays, after 10 months of training): do the same sessions but stop every set about 3 reps
  short of failure and skip the last set of each exercise; return to full sessions in week 2.
- **Block 9 — Hypertrophy Reverse Pyramid III (1 Feb 2027).** Reverse pyramids 8-10-12-15 (A) /
  10-12-15-20 (B) and drop sets 12+12 (A) / 15+15 (B), using the strength from block 7. New: flat
  barbell bench, barbell T-bar row, standing overhead press, front squat, Smith hip thrust.

## How to use it in the app

Pick the active block in "Planned before you go". Each week, follow days 1–6 in order (A days
first, then B days). After six weeks, switch to the next block. If you miss a day, continue with the
next one in order rather than skipping it.

Any sharp pain during an exercise is a signal to stop that exercise and see a qualified
professional; this plan is general training guidance, not medical advice.
