## Summary

**Short answer: no, it was not right. The lateral raises on Chest-Back days were my mistake.**

- They meant side delts were trained 4 days a week, on two pairs of back-to-back days (Mon then Tue, Thu then Fri).
- The day name didn't say so.
- They added nothing that Arms day couldn't do.

In v2, **all shoulder work is on the Shoulders & Arms days**, with the same weekly volume. Chest & Back days are only chest, back and abs.

The split itself stays. Each muscle is trained twice a week, no session goes above 10 sets for one muscle, and he has trained this split for 33 weeks. Changing to upper/lower or push/pull/legs the night before he starts would cost more than it gains.

## Week timeline and muscle map

| Day | Session | Primary | Secondary | Recovery check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Chest & Back A | Chest, lats/upper back, abs | Front delts, triceps, biceps, rear delts | — |
| Tue | Shoulders & Arms A | Side + rear delts, biceps, triceps | Front delts (press) | 24 h after Mon's pressing and pulling, but only indirect overlap. The press goes first while he is fresh |

**What still overlaps, and why I accept it**

- Front delts, triceps and biceps get indirect work on Monday and direct work on Tuesday. Every muscle still has 72 h between its two direct sessions.
- Weekly volume stays inside the evidence-brief ranges, and frequency matters little once volume is equal.
- Elbows: 7 curl sets plus 4 overhead extension sets per arm day. This is the same load as v1 and moderate.
- ⚠️ REVISAR: if his shoulders or elbows feel beaten up on Tuesdays, the first fix is to drop the Smith press to 2 sets. Sharp or lasting pain means stopping that exercise and seeing a professional.

## What changed and why (honest list)

1. **Laterals moved off Chest & Back days.** The standing DB lateral is now on arm days, in a superset with the incline curl. Both use dumbbells at one bench.
2. **Abs moved to Chest & Back days.** Arm days would have been too long. This also keeps leg days short, since leg days are the ones he skips.
3. **Biceps priority.** Incline curls now come right after the press, before the delt isolation work. The preacher curl goes from 3 to 4 sets and the incline curl from 4 to 3, so biceps are still 20 weekly sets, the top of the range. At maintenance calories I kept every volume as it was.
4. **Busy-gym supersets.**
   - The machine pullover is replaced by the **cable straight-arm pulldown** (`cable-straight-arms-pull-downs`). It uses the same cable tower as the fly, and the crunch uses it too.
   - The cable lateral is paired with the overhead rope extension, so both use the same low cable.
   - Key tips now say: if a machine is taken, do both exercises as straight sets.
5. **Counting fixes in v1.**
   - Triceps were over-counted. The pullover adds almost nothing, so the real total is about 17, not 20.
   - My back target said 12–16 while the plan prescribed 18. 18 is fine, and inside the brief's 12–20.
6. **Unchanged:** the legs days, the main lifts, the angles, the effort ramp and the lighter week 6. ⚠️ REVISAR: week 6 is still open. Sebas did not answer the lighter-week question.

**Session length** (incl. ~8 min warm-up): Chest & Back ~55 min, Shoulders & Arms ~55 min, Legs ~60 min.
