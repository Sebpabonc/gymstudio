## Summary
Personal guidance for Sebas for the first 2-3 weeks of Block 7 (Strength · Heavy Sets of 6-8 II, starts Mon
16 Nov), based on his real Block 6 data. It stays inside the approved block text, the strategy ("if you miss a day,
continue with the next one in order") and the approved engine rules (R4 deload, R6 incomplete session, R11 long
break). It adds no app rule. Anything that would change the plan or the app is listed under Open decisions as a
proposal.

## Guidance (as given to Sebas)

**1. What the data says.** Block 6 was not a standstill. Four lifts set new bests in weeks 4-5: T-bar row
30 → 42.5 kg × 12, straight-arm pulldown 22.5 → 32.5 kg × 12, rope overhead triceps 20 → 30 kg × 12, cable fly
12.5 × 13 → 20 × 10. Others are holding, and some of those are about the same strength as their best: bench
37.5 × 10 is roughly equal to 40 × 8. Most holding lifts started around 26 Oct, the week sessions went from 6 to
3-4 and weekly sets from about 130 to about 50. Fewer sessions and sets usually means slower progress; it does not
mean the plan stopped working. Lateral raises (12 kg) and rear delt flies (6 kg) use small dumbbells, where one
2 kg step is a big jump, so they progress by reps first.

**2. This week.** About 4 days off after a deload costs no real strength. Follow week 1 exactly as written: no top
set (do it as a back-off set) and no extras. Most exercises are new, so each first session is about finding your
weight: 3-4 warm-up sets, then stop every working set with 2-3 clean reps left. Use the app's suggestion or less,
never more.

**3. Fitting it into your week.** The plan already says: if you miss a day, do the next day in order; don't skip
ahead. So if you train Mon, Tue, Thu, Thursday is Lower Body A. Never skip a leg day to get to an upper day.
Combining days or a 4-day version would change the plan, so that is a separate proposal.

**4. Legs after 4 weeks off.** You last squatted 90 × 8 on 15 Oct and 85 × 10 on 18 Oct. After a 3-8 week
break the app takes about 10% off your last weights for the back squat and the RDL; accept it, even if it feels
light. Set the safety pins. Warm up with the empty bar × 10, then add about 10 kg per set for 5 reps up to your
first working set. Stop every set with 2-3 clean reps left. Week 2 builds from there. Expect sore legs for 1-3 days after the first session; that is normal and
usually much less after the second. Sharp or joint pain, or feeling unwell: stop and see a qualified professional.

**5. Signals in Progress.**
- Consistency: Lower Body A and B both logged in each plan week.
- Lifts: "Too early to tell" in weeks 1-2 is expected (first sessions are your baseline). From week 3, look for
  "New best recently" on the main lifts.
- The Est. max line for back squat and Smith bench heading up by week 5.

## Exercises

### Back Squat (`back-squat`)
- **Notes:** A1 on both leg days (A: reverse pyramid 5-6-8-10, week 1 without the top set; B: 4 × 8). Ramp-up as
  above; safety pins every session. Posture tips: see the approved catalogue entry.

### Romanian Deadlift (`romanian-deadlift`)
- **Notes:** Last logged 18 Oct, so from 16 Nov it is 29 days, inside the engine's 22-56 day band (R11, -10%). Block 7
  reps are 8 (A) / 10 (B) vs 10-12 in Block 6.

### Smith Machine Bench Press (`smith-machine-bench-press`), Seated Barbell Overhead Press (`barbell-overhead-press-seated`)
- **Notes:** A-day reverse pyramids; week 1 = no top set. Week 2 is the first top set: 1-2 reps in reserve, never
  grind (approved block text).

### Lifts referenced from Block 6 data
- `incline-dumbbell-press` (holding at 32 × 8, now in Block 7 C1), `standing-db-lateral-raises` (12 kg, small-step
  lift, engine R8). No new exercises proposed.
