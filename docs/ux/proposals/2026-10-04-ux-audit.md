# UX audit — GymStudio on the phone

> Proposal only. Nothing here is committed work until Sebas approves it. Fitness content
> (aliases, cues, rest rules) goes to the PT; architecture and cost go to the Tech Lead.
> **Already shipped or in progress, so not proposed again here:** greyed-out "completed" exercise
> state with day-tab progress (#65, live), combined superset logging (#64), **prefilled weights
> from the last session**, **visible logging errors**, and the QA agent. Proposals below build on
> them where noted.

## 1. Summary — top 10, ranked

Ranked by impact on the main job (logging a workout mid-session) divided by effort.

| # | Recommendation | Why (one line) | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Faster set entry**: one-row sets with ± steppers, a ✓ per set, "same as set 1" copy, bigger inputs (on top of the in-flight prefill) | Reaching an input takes 3 taps on 28 px buttons, inputs are ~39 px tall, and each value is typed with the keypad | M |
| 2 | **Open on "Today"**: the app opens on the plan, on the next unlogged day of the current block | The app opens on Barbell Bench Press in the free-log tab. The plan shows Block 6 but always opens on Day 1, with no "today" marker | M |
| 3 | **Bottom tab bar + compact header** | The header uses about 200 px (25% of the screen) on every tab, and the tabs sit at the top, out of thumb reach | S–M |
| 4 | **Rest timer that starts itself** when a set is ticked, using the prescribed rest | Each exercise already shows "Rest 90 s", but there is no timer; users leave the app to time rest | M |
| 5 | **Feedback after logging**: success toast with Undo, PR badge on the spot, end-of-session summary | Success is only shown by the card collapsing to "Done". A new PR only shows up later in Progress, and there's no session wrap-up | S |
| 6 | **Progress: lead with one headline and make suggestions actionable** ("why" + "use next time") | Progress opens on five near-identical "Add weight" cards with no reason and no link; they still point at a block that has ended | M |
| 7 | **Simpler block switching**: current block chosen by date, compact chooser in a bottom sheet | "Change block" is hidden behind an expand button and opens 9 blocks with a paragraph each. Status labels ("Current" vs "Completed") contradicted each other on the day before Block 6 | S |
| 8 | **Smarter exercise search**: abbreviations and spacing variants (RDL, OHP, "lat pull down") | Common gym words return "No exercises found" | S (+PT aliases) |
| 9 | **Gym-proof touch targets and labels**: 44–48 px controls, plain labels instead of slugs | Many controls are 25–34 px tall; "reverse-pyramid" and "Chest · Chest" are shown as-is | S |
| 10 | **First-run welcome and account value**: one optional card, and a sign-in screen that says what you gain | First-time users get no orientation; the sign-in screen says "Welcome back" even to new users and does not explain backup/sync | S |

**Bug found during the audit (for Tech Lead / QAer, not a UX proposal):** opening the account
screen on the live site produced a **completely blank app**. The console showed a 404 for the
lazily loaded `ProfileScreen-*.js` chunk (a stale chunk after a deploy). The app has no error
boundary, so one failed chunk blanks the whole screen. A reload fixed it. Suggested fix: an error
boundary with a "Reload app" button around lazy screens, plus retry on chunk-load failure.

## 2. Method

- **App walk-through** (4 Oct 2026) on `https://sebpabonc.github.io/gymstudio/?demo=1` at 375 × 812
  (phone emulation), in a separate browser tab. Pages were read with screenshots, page text and
  read-only DOM queries (element sizes, labels, input types). A first, interrupted pass logged a
  few demo-mode sets (demo data only, never the real account). The **verification pass on the
  same day logged nothing** and only read pages. I never signed in. The browser was shared with
  other sessions on the same site, and demo history changed while I was reading (Day 1 went from
  2/7 to 3/7 without my input), so I stopped interacting. Where a finding comes from the first
  pass and could not be re-checked, it says so.
- **Jobs walked:** (a) find today's planned workout and log an exercise and a superset;
  (b) look up an exercise and its tips; (c) understand Progress; (d) switch training block;
  (e) open the account / sign-in screen (read from source code `src/screens/LoginScreen.tsx`
  after the live screen failed to load, see bug above).
- **Docs read:** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/architecture/auth-and-sync.md`,
  `docs/fitness/approved/progress-insights.md`, `docs/fitness/approved/training-blocks/strategy.md`.
  There are no QA reports yet.
- **Research:** competitor apps (Strong, Hevy, Fitbod, JEFIT, Future, Ladder, Apple Watch/Fitness,
  WHOOP, Strava, Fitbit) and guidelines (NN/g, Apple HIG, Material 3). Links are in §8.
- **Gym-context lens:** used with one hand, sweaty fingers, quick glances between sets, 60–120 s
  rest windows, phone often on a bench or in a pocket.

| # | Evidence | Problem |

### Cross-cutting

- **Touch targets:** chips 27 px, expand buttons 25–28 px, "Exit demo" 27 px, account 34 px,
  inputs 36–39 px. Apple recommends at least 44 pt and NN/g recommends about 1 cm × 1 cm. Sweaty
  hands make small targets worse.
- **Visual hierarchy:** almost every heading is small uppercase letter-spaced text, so section
  titles, labels and buttons look alike. The dark theme and warm accent look premium. Keep them.
- **Consistency:** "Track it as you go" / "Planned before you go" / "Make your plan" mix three
  naming styles. "Save workout" (free log) and "Log this exercise" (plan) do the same thing with
  different words.
- **Onboarding:** none. A new user doesn't know what a block, A/B day or reverse pyramid is until
  they open "About this block". The demo banner is a good pattern.

## 4. Proposals

### P1. Faster set entry (steppers, tick per set, copy) — **M**

**Problem:** a5, a6, a7. Three taps on 28 px buttons to reach a ~39 px input, then the keypad
for every change. **Builds on** the in-flight prefill (last session's weights) and #64; it does
not replace them.

**Proposal**
1. Expanding an exercise opens the set log directly (no second "Set log +" tap).
2. One compact row per set: `Set 1 | − 44 + kg | − 15 + reps | ✓`, 48 px tall. Drop sets get a
   second, indented row.
3. **± steppers** (weight step from the equipment, e.g. 2 kg dumbbells / 2.5 kg plates, PT/Tech
   Lead to confirm; reps step 1). Tapping the number still opens the keypad (`inputmode="decimal"`).
4. A **✓ button per set** marks it done (this also feeds P4's rest timer). Editing set 1 copies the
   new weight into later sets that haven't been touched ("same weight every set" in Block 6).
5. Optional "+2.5" chip when Progress says "ready to add weight" (tap to apply; see Decision 3).
6. "Log this exercise" becomes "Finish exercise" and is only needed if not all sets were ticked.

```
┌ Cable Fly · B1 ──────────── 3 × 15+15 · rest 90 s ┐
│ Last time (24 Sept, B day): 44 → 32 kg × 15+15    │
│ Set 1  [−] 44 [+] kg   [−] 15 [+]   ( ✓ )          │
│   drop [−] 32 [+] kg   [−] 15 [+]                  │
│ Set 2  [−] 44 [+] kg   [−] 15 [+]   ( ✓ )          │
│ Set 3  [−] 44 [+] kg   [−] 15 [+]   ( ✓ )          │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Impact:** high. With prefill, a 4-set exercise goes from 3 taps + keypad per change to 4 ticks
when nothing changes, and one stepper tap when it does.
**Dependencies:** in-flight prefill; Tech Lead decides whether the per-set "done" state is stored
(data-model change). Must fit with #64 (superset combined log) and the completed state (#65).
**Risks:** prefilled values get logged without real checking. Mitigation: untouched values look
visibly "ghosted" until ✓ is tapped, which keeps the "don't save untouched rows as 0 kg" rule
(#49) intact.
**Success metric:** median time from opening an exercise to logging it −40%. Fewer than 10% of
logged sets need editing afterwards.

### P2. Open on "Today" — **M**

**Problem:** a1, a2, a3, d1.

**Proposal**
1. On launch, pick the **current block by date** (`start_date ≤ today < start + 6 weeks`). If none
   is current, show the most recent block and a "Next block starts Mon 5 Oct" banner.
2. Pick **today's day** = the first day of the current block week that hasn't been logged yet,
   following the order 1→6 (the strategy rule "if you miss a day, continue with the next one").
   Mark it with a **"Today"** pill on the day selector. Other days stay one tap away.
3. The first tab becomes **Today**: block name and week ("Block 6 · Week 1 of 6"), today's day
   title, the exercise list, and a progress line ("0 of 7 exercises").
4. On the first day of a new block, show a single card: "Block 6 starts today — Flat Pyramid II.
   [See what's new] [Start]".

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Block 6 · Week 1 of 6    Mon 5 Oct   │
│ TODAY  Day 1 · Chest-Back A          │
│ Chest & back · heavy · ~60 min       │
│ ▸ 0 of 7 exercises done              │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ A1 Dumbbell Bench Press · 30°   4×10 │
│ B1+B2 DB Press 0° + Cable Fly  3×10/12│
│ …                                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Impact:** high. It removes navigation and memory work at the start of every session.
**Dependencies:** Tech Lead (selection logic, timezone; history already stores `blockId`/`dayKey`).
**Risks:** wrong guess when sessions are logged out of order. Mitigation: always show the guess
as a removable pill, never lock the selection, and remember a manual choice for the rest of the day.
**Success metric:** at least 90% of sessions start without changing day or block. Time to first
logged set under 20 s.

### P3. Bottom tab bar and compact header — **S–M**

**Problem:** a4, a11, e1, inconsistent tab names.

**Proposal**
1. Bottom navigation with 4 destinations, icon plus short label: **Today · Exercises · Progress ·
   You**. "You" holds account, sync status, settings and the demo toggle.
2. Fold "Make your plan" into **Exercises** ("Build a custom day") or a "…" menu on Today. It is
   a secondary job next to PT-designed blocks.
3. The header shrinks to one 48–56 px row (screen title on the left, sync dot on the right). The
   brand row and subtitle appear only on Today and scroll away.
4. Bottom bar items at least 48 px tall, with safe-area padding for iOS.

```
┌ Today            ● synced ┐
│                            │
│   …content…                │
│                            │
├────┬─────────┬──────┬─────┤
│ ⌂  │   🔍    │  📈  │  👤 │
│Today│Exercises│Progress│You│
└────┴─────────┴──────┴─────┘
```

**Impact:** high on reach and screen space. About 150 px more content on every screen.
**Dependencies:** none beyond design. Check against the Capacitor iOS safe areas.
**Risks:** re-learning cost for Sebas (the only current user). Low.
**Success metric:** visible content height +25%. No wrong-tab taps in QA walk-throughs.

### P4. Automatic rest timer — **M**

**Problem:** a9.

**Proposal**
1. Ticking a set (P1) starts a countdown from the exercise's prescribed rest (90 s, 2 min…). In a
   superset, ticking B1 starts the short gap ("10 s → B2") and ticking B2 starts the full rest.
2. The timer lives in a **sticky bar above the bottom navigation**:
   `Rest 1:12  [−15] [+15] [Skip]`, with large digits readable at arm's length.
3. At 0: vibration (Capacitor haptics on iOS, the Vibration API where supported) and an optional
   sound. The bar turns into "Next: Set 3 · 44 kg × 15".
4. It keeps counting if the user switches tabs or locks the phone (store an end timestamp, not
   a counter).

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│  REST  1:12     [−15] [+15]  [Skip]  │
│  Next: Cable Fly · set 3 · 44 × 15   │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Impact:** high gym-context value. It keeps the user in the app between sets, which also opens
room for a glanceable cue ("Next set cue: squeeze at the bottom").
**Dependencies:** P1 (✓ per set), #64 for supersets. Tech Lead decides native notification vs
in-app only (web limits background sounds).
**Risks:** annoying sounds. Make the sound opt-in and vibration on by default.
**Success metric:** at least 60% of logged sets have a completed or skipped timer. Self-reported
"I use another app to time rest" drops to zero.

### P5. Feedback after logging — **S**

**Problem:** a8.

**Proposal**
1. After finishing an exercise: a toast at the bottom, "Cable Fly logged · 3 sets · **New weight
   PR 45 kg** 🎉 [Undo]", visible for 5 s and announced via `aria-live`.
2. The exercise card collapses into its "Done" state (#65, live) and adds a PR badge. Error
   messages stay with the in-flight "visible logging errors" work; this proposal is the success side.
3. When the last exercise of the day is logged, show a **session summary** sheet: duration, sets,
   volume, PRs, "Next session: Day 2 · Arms A (Tue)". This is the anchor for AI Phase 1's
   post-workout summary (see the AI roadmap).

**Impact:** medium-high on confidence and motivation. **Effort:** S.
**Dependencies:** PR logic already exists in `src/progress`.
**Risks:** too many toasts. Show one per exercise, not per set.
**Success metric:** zero "did it save?" duplicate logs (same exercise logged twice within 2 min).

### P6. Progress: a headline first, suggestions you can act on — **M**

**Problem:** c1–c7.

**Proposal**
1. Top card **"This week"**: sessions done/planned (dots labelled Mon–Sun or D1–D6), PRs this
   week, and one generated sentence ("On track: 1 of 6 sessions, 1 PR").
2. **Suggestions** grouped and made actionable:
   - One card: "Ready to add weight on 5 lifts" → expands to the list.
   - Each row has a **"Why?"** line ("You hit 15+15 on all 3 sets last two B days") and a
     **"Use next time"** action that sets the next session's prefill (in-flight prefill work).
   - Suggestions for a block that has ended are hidden. At a block boundary, show "Block 5 report
     is ready" instead.
3. **Strength chart:** exercise picker as a searchable bottom sheet grouped by "In your current
   block" first, then the rest A–Z. Add "kg" and dates on the axes. Break the line where a block
   has no data.
4. Plain labels everywhere: "Reverse pyramid" instead of `reverse-pyramid`. Weekly sets status
   becomes "Above range (planned)" with a neutral colour. Add an ⓘ tooltip for e1RM ("estimated
   1-rep max").
5. Collapse all cards except the headline and the chart by default (progressive disclosure).

**Impact:** high for "am I progressing?" at a glance. **Dependencies:** PT to confirm the
wording of the "why" templates (they're already in `progress-insights.md`). Tech Lead for the
block-boundary rule.
**Risks:** low.
**Success metric:** in a 5-second test, the user can say whether they are progressing. At least
30% of suggestions get "Use next time".

### P7. Simpler block switching — **S**

**Proposal:** auto-select by date (P2). "Change block" becomes a visible link on the block card
that opens a **bottom sheet** with one line per block ("6 · Flat Pyramid II · 5 Oct–15 Nov ·
Now"). Descriptions only show for the selected row. Replace "Coach"/"PT" chips with "Designed by
your coach" / "Designed by the PT" in the About section.
**Success metric:** switching block takes 2 taps or fewer and causes no confusion in QA walk-throughs.

### P8. Smarter exercise search — **S** (+ PT content)

**Proposal:** normalise spaces and hyphens ("pull down" = "pulldown"), match each word in any
order, and add an **alias list** per exercise (RDL, OHP, SLDL, skull crusher/lying triceps
extension, pec deck/butterfly). The PT drafts the aliases in the catalogue (approved flow). Fix
"Chest · Chest" by hiding the secondary muscle when it equals the category.
**Success metric:** zero "No exercises found" for a test list of 30 common gym terms.

### P9. Gym-proof targets and labels — **S**

**Proposal:** a design-system pass. Every tappable element is at least 44 × 44 pt (48 px
preferred for the main logging controls). Expand/collapse uses the whole card header as the hit
area, not just the 28 px "+". Use sentence-case labels for buttons, so uppercase is kept for small
section labels only. Rename the tabs (P3). Use "Log" consistently.
**Success metric:** an accessibility audit (axe target-size) passes. QAer checklist item added.

### P10. First-run welcome and account value — **S**

**Proposal**
1. First launch only: one dismissible card on Today, "Welcome to GymStudio. You're on Block 6:
   6 sessions a week, A days heavy, B days lighter. Tap an exercise to log it. [Got it] [Try the
   demo]". There's no carousel (NN/g: tutorials are mostly skipped and don't speed users up).
2. Contextual ⓘ next to jargon the first time it appears (A/B day, reverse pyramid, drop set,
   e1RM). The text comes from the existing approved strategy.
3. Sign-in screen: when no local history exists, default to **Create account**. Add 3 benefit
   lines above the form ("Back up your workouts", "Use it on any device", "Your data is private to
   you") and a link to a short privacy note.
4. Account entry in the bottom bar ("You") with a text label.

**Success metric:** new users log their first set within 2 minutes. More sign-ups among users
with 3 or more sessions.

## 6. Decisions for Sebas

1. **Navigation:** OK to move the tabs to a bottom bar named *Today · Exercises · Progress · You*,
   and to move "Make your plan" inside Exercises?
2. **Auto-pick today's workout:** should the app choose the block by date and the next unlogged
   day automatically (you can still change it)? Yes / no.
3. **Suggested weight:** when Progress says "ready to add 2.5 kg", should the set row show a
   "+2.5" chip you tap to accept (recommended), or apply it automatically on top of last time's
   prefilled weight?
4. **Rest timer:** vibration on by default and sound off by default. OK?
5. **Order of work:** quick wins first (bug fix, bottom bar, bigger targets, toast), then P1/P2.
   Or start with P1 (fast logging) together with #64?
