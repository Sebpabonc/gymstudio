AI help button

Prototype for Sebas. A round AI button sits on its own to the right of the menu bar on every screen. Tap it to open one window with two options: ask the AI anything about training, or send a comment about the app. Tap the round button at the bottom right of the phone. The two tabs, the suggested questions and Send all work (answers are examples).

GYM STUDIO

How it works

  • Where: a 60 px round glass button, alone at the bottom right next to the menu bar, on every tab. It hides while you type, like the menu bar.
  • Ask AI: one chat for any training question (not tied to a single exercise). The AI knows your current block, today's day and your recent sessions. Same limits as Ask AI today (20 questions a day, USD 15 a month), no medical advice, answers in your language.
  • Send a comment: pick a type (Problem, Idea, Exercise or content, Other), write it and send. It saves with the app version, screen and language so the team can act on it. You get a "Thanks" right away.
  • Where comments go: a new table in the app's database. The Tech Lead turns them into tasks for the team (bugs to QAer/Copilot, content to the PT).
  • Exercise "Ask AI" buttons stay inside each exercise for questions about that exercise.
