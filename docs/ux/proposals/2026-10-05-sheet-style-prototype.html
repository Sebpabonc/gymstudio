The only change is that content sits directly on one rounded sheet (rows and hairline dividers) instead of boxes inside boxes. Use the toggle to compare. Colours: “Darker” (default here) previews deeper surfaces — background #0f0f10, cards/sheet #171719 — against today's greys (#1f1f20 / #2b2b2d); text and accent colours are unchanged.

What changes

  • Boxes → one sheet. Below the GYM STUDIO header, a single surface (--bg-soft #2b2b2d, an existing token) with a 44 px top radius overlaps the header by 22 px. All content sits on it.
  • Rows, not cards. Sections and exercises are separated by 1 px hairlines (rgba(255,255,255,.08), the dock's border value) and 16 px gutters. Today goes from up to 6 nested boxes to 1 level.
  • Superset keeps its mauve identity as a side rail (--nav-active) plus a faint tint, with no border box.
  • Open exercise is a faint full-width band; the set table, squeeze cue (tint, no border), tips, progress and Ask AI sit on it as rows.
  • Inputs, buttons, chips, the "Log rounds" panel and tick buttons keep their own outlines: they are controls, not containers.

What does not change

  • Dark theme and every colour token, Inter, the current type sizes (PO decision), the collapsing header, the floating dock, the rest timer pill, set table columns.
