Exercise name size

Prototype for Sebas: the Planned day (Day 1 · Chest & Back A, Block 6) at three sizes for the exercise name only. Everything else (codes, details, chips, cue, colours) stays exactly as in the app today. Measured in the live app at 390 px: a closed exercise name is 12.8 px bold and it grows to 17 px when you open it — that jump is what you see today.

Tapping the name opens Google Images. The icon only hints at that; pick the one you like (Arrow is the usual "opens outside" sign). Larger sizes wrap long names to two lines rather than cutting them off.
