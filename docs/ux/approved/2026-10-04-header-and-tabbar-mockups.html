Header stays 56 px (same as PR #86): wordmark left, screen name as a small uppercase subtitle after a hairline divider, sync dot right. Bar: 4 thin-line icons, no labels, no pill; active = lighter brand tint + heavier stroke + 4 px dot. Translucent blurred background with a faint hairline.

Pros

  • Brand back at zero height cost; the most minimal bar (about 10 px shorter than today's).
  • Active state uses three cues (colour, stroke weight, dot), so it is not colour-only.
  • Smallest code change: CSS on .brand-header and .bottom-tab-bar, one span in the markup.

Cons

  • No visible labels; NN/g research shows unlabelled icons are understood less well by new users.
  • The wordmark is smaller (19 px) than the old 2 rem hero version.
  • Needs aria-labels and long-press or tooltip hints so the tabs stay discoverable.

Hero header (about 110 px) brings back the original wordmark and tagline, then shrinks to a 48 px bar once the user scrolls, like iOS large titles. Bar keeps icons with tiny 10 px labels; active = brand tint + bold label, no pill, no separate panel.

Pros

Cons

  • Takes about 55 px more at the top until the user scrolls, so less of the first exercise is visible mid-session.
  • Needs scroll-linked JS or CSS and a reduced-motion fallback, so it is the most work to build.
  • The bar is less minimal than Option 1 because labels add visual noise.

Same 56 px wordmark header as Option 1. A 228 × 60 px rounded dock floats 26 px above the home indicator over the content (blur + soft shadow). Content scrolls under it and gets extra bottom padding.

Pros

  • The most modern and "designed" look; matches the iOS 26 floating tab bar direction.
  • Content uses the full height; no full-width bar edge.
  • Pairs well with the rounded cards already in the app (22 px radius).

Cons

  • Covers the "+" buttons and set inputs at the bottom of the list; needs careful padding, which is risky for mid-set logging.
  • Blur over busy content can reduce legibility; needs a solid fallback.
  • No labels (same discoverability issue as Option 1) and a compact 50 px hit area.

Accessibility (applies to all options)

  • Tap targets: each tab is at least 50 × 48 px (Apple asks for 44 pt and Material for 48 dp). With 4 tabs, Option 1 gives about 88 × 50 px per tab.
  • The active state is never colour-only. It combines colour, a heavier stroke and a dot (Options 1 and 3), or colour and a bold label (Option 2). Keep aria-current="page".
  • Icon-only tabs need aria-label="Today" and similar on each button, with aria-hidden on the SVG. Keep <nav aria-label="Primary navigation">.
  • Contrast: active #c99ca1 on #1f1f20 is about 6.9:1, and inactive at 55% white is about 5:1. The existing --brand (#8d5e63) is only about 3:1, so do not use it for icons.
  • The wordmark is decorative branding. Keep one real heading per screen: visually-hidden <h1>Today</h1>, or make the screen subtitle the h1.
  • Option 2: respect prefers-reduced-motion by switching states without an animation.

What changed vs. today (PR #86)

  • Header: "Today" alone becomes the GYM STUDIO wordmark plus a "TODAY" subtitle, at the same 56 px in Options 1 and 3.
  • Bar: the active pill background and the solid dark panel are removed. Options 1 and 3 also drop the labels.
  • Icon glyphs stay the same (home, search, chart, person). Only the stroke weight and colour change.
