GymStudio support

Contact: gymstudioapp@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 5 business days. Tell us your device, iOS version (or browser) and what you were doing. Never send us your password.

Signed in, you can also write to us from the app: tap the Help and AI button, then Send feedback.

Medical emergencies: we can't help. Call your local emergency number.

Delete your account

You can delete your account inside the app:

  1. Sign in and open You.
  2. Under Delete account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Delete permanently.

Your account and all its synced data are deleted at once, and this cannot be undone. The app then signs you out on that phone and removes that account's data from it. Workouts saved on the phone without signing in (as a guest) stay on the phone.

Can't sign in? Write to gymstudioapp@gmail.com from your GymStudio account's email address (for Google sign-in, your Google email; for Sign in with Apple with Hide My Email, include the relay address ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com that Apple shows for GymStudio in your Apple Account settings), with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm the request, to make sure it comes from you. We delete your account and all its data within 10 days and email you when it is done.

Removing the app alone does not delete your account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Turn AI off

AI is optional and off until you tap Allow AI. To turn it off later, go to You → Privacy & AI and switch off AI features (OpenAI). The app keeps working with rule-based reasons and plans. See the Privacy Policy for what AI sends to OpenAI.

Common questions

Do I need an account? Not to try GymStudio; what you log then stays on your device. Plans, goals, measurements, sync, feedback and AI need an account. You can sign up with email or with Google, and in the iPhone app also with Sign in with Apple.

Does it work offline? Logging does, and your data syncs when you reconnect. Sign-in, new plans and AI need a connection.

I didn't get the confirmation or reset email. It comes from gymstudioapp@gmail.com. Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes. Still nothing? Write to us.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?. We email you a link to set a new password.

How is today's weight chosen? The app's rules use your recent sets. Change it if it doesn't feel right. You decide what you lift.

Is a person coaching me? No. GymStudio's AI trainer is software. No human trainer or health professional reviews or supervises your plan. The app's rules and plan templates were prepared with an AI specialist agent and approved by the app's owner.

Is this medical advice? No. Check with a doctor before starting a program, and stop if you feel pain or unwell.

Why doesn't the timer show on my Lock Screen? Live Activities need an iPhone with iOS 16.2 or later, and they must be allowed for the app in iOS Settings. The Dynamic Island appears on iPhone models that have it.

Which devices are supported? iPhone with iOS 15 or later, and the web app at https://sebpabonc.github.io/gymstudio/ in a current browser. There is no iPad version.

Is GymStudio free? Yes. There are no payments or subscriptions in the app.

Who can use it? People aged 18 or older.
