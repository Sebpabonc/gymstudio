GymStudio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what GymStudio collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and how you can control it. GymStudio is a training app for looking up exercises, following a plan, logging sets and seeing your progress. It is available as an iPhone app and as a web app at https://sebpabonc.github.io/gymstudio/.

1. Who is responsible

GymStudio is run by Sebastian Pabon, an individual based in Australia, not a company. He is responsible for your personal data ("we", "us"). Privacy contact: gymstudioapp@gmail.com.

2. Using GymStudio without an account

You can try GymStudio without an account. Anything you log then stays on your device. When the app downloads the exercise list, our hosting provider receives your IP address as part of the connection. Plans, goals, measurements, feedback, sync and AI features need an account.

GymStudio is for people aged 18 or older. The iPhone app is not offered in the European Union.

All of this is optional except your email address (and a password if you don't use Google). You can use GymStudio without entering body weight, steps, calories or goal notes.

We do not use Apple Health, your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. GymStudio has no ads, no analytics tools and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your data. We do not use your health and fitness data for advertising or marketing.

On your device, the app also stores your data, preferences and sign-in session so it can work offline. During a rest, the timer and the exercise name can appear on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island through Live Activities. This stays on your device.

5. AI features

Some GymStudio features use OpenAI, a company in the United States, to write text. AI is optional. Nothing is sent to OpenAI until you tap "Allow AI" in the app. If you choose "Not now", or turn AI off later, the app does not send anything to OpenAI.

The app uses AI in three places:

FeatureWhenWhat is sent to OpenAI
Ask AI (Help and AI button)When you ask a questionYour question, today's date, your current plan, and your 30 most recent workout entries (exercise, date, and each set's weight, reps and related set details)
AI Trainer explanationAutomatically, when the app shows a recommended loadThe exercise name; the recommended and previous load and reps; your last session's date, weight, reps and target; how many days ago it was; your estimated one-rep maximum; how many sessions were used
Plan personalisationWhen you generate a planYour goal, days per week, experience, activity level, session length and equipment; your 14 most recent body measurement entries (date, body weight, steps, calories); your plan's days, exercises, sets and rest times; a list of alternative exercises from the app's library

Never sent to OpenAI: your name, your email address, your account ID, your workout notes or your goal notes. The request goes from our server, so OpenAI does not see your IP address. Please avoid typing names or medical details into AI questions.

OpenAI says that data sent through its API is not used to train its models unless the customer chooses to share it, and that it may keep the data for up to 30 days to detect abuse, or longer where the law requires.

The load shown each day is calculated by the app's rules. The AI writes the explanation, and it can personalise a plan only inside limits set by those rules. The AI trainer is software, not a person, and AI answers can be wrong.

To turn AI off: in the app, go to You → Privacy & AI and switch off "AI features (OpenAI)". Your choice is saved on each device you use, so a new device asks you again. Data already sent cannot be recalled; OpenAI keeps it as described above.

8. How to delete your account

Today, account deletion works by email:

  1. Write to gymstudioapp@gmail.com from the email address of your GymStudio account (for Google sign-in, your Google email), with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. We may ask you to confirm the request, to make sure it comes from you.
  3. We delete your account and all its data from our database within 10 days, and email you when it is done.

Copies in backups and logs expire as described in section 7. Data on your devices stays until you remove the app there. Removing the app alone does not delete your account. We are adding a delete button inside the app; this section will be updated when it is available.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see your data, get a copy, correct it, delete it, limit or object to how it is used, find out who received it, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier use. You can correct most data in the app. For everything else, write to gymstudioapp@gmail.com. We answer within 10 business days, or sooner if your local law sets a shorter period. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why, and you can ask us to review that decision. We won't treat you worse for using your rights.

You can also complain to your data protection or consumer authority. Examples: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (Australia), Personal Data Protection Commission (Singapore), Personal Information Protection Commission (Japan and South Korea), Data Protection Board (India), Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (Colombia), Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (Mexico), Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (Argentina), your state attorney general or the Federal Trade Commission (United States).

10. Children

GymStudio is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from younger people. If you think a younger person has an account, write to us and we will delete it and its data.

11. Security

Connections are encrypted. Each person's data is kept separate in the database by access rules, so other users cannot see it. AI keys and administrator keys stay on our server and are never in the app. No system is fully secure. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

12. If you live in the United States

We treat your body weight, steps, calories and training goals as consumer health data. We collect it only from you, use it only to provide the features you use (your plan, recommendations and progress), and share it only with the providers in section 6 for that purpose, and with OpenAI only if you allow AI. We do not sell it or use it for targeted advertising. To see, delete or stop sharing it, write to gymstudioapp@gmail.com; if we refuse, you can ask us to review the decision, and then contact your state attorney general.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date, and if a change is important, tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. If we want to use your data for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

14. Language

This policy is available in English and Spanish. If you use the app in Spanish or live in a Spanish-speaking country, the Spanish version applies. Otherwise, the English version applies.
